Buzzheavier Client for Windows
https://github.com/uzergit/Buzzheavier-Client

Start
  Double-click BuzzheavierClient.exe.
  You can also drag one or more files onto BuzzheavierClient.exe to upload
  them straight away.

  Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time,
  because the app is not signed. Click "More info" and then "Run anyway".

Uploading to your account
  Copy your Account ID from https://buzzheavier.com/settings and paste it in
  Settings -> 1. Settings from version 1 (buzz_settings.ini next to the .exe)
  are imported automatically.

Command line
  BuzzheavierClient.exe --help

Settings and upload history are stored in %APPDATA%\BuzzheavierClient
